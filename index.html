Risk disclaimer & evaluation framework

Read the filters: Supertrend shows trend direction. ADX measures strength, while +DI and -DI show directional movement. A directional outlook needs ADX at least 20 and matching DI when this filter is on.

Know the limits: Sideways markets can cause false signals. Rapid moves, gaps and slippage can make an actual trade differ from the chart. This view does not measure returns or account for trading costs.

  1. Choose the instrument, timeframe, entry, exit and maximum risk before reviewing results.
  2. Record every signal on a closed candle, including signals the filters reject.
  3. Compare outcomes after costs across many sessions. Track sample size, win rate, average gain and loss, and largest drawdown.
  4. Check later, unseen sessions before relying on any apparent pattern. Past results cannot guarantee future returns.
